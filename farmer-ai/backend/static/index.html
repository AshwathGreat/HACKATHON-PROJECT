The journey

IDENTIFY → ACT → RECOVER

KisanGo guides farmers through three structured steps — from spotting a disease to claiming government support.

1
📸

Photograph & Identify

Upload or speak to describe the affected leaf. Our MobileNetV3 AI model identifies the disease and flags uncertain results for expert review.

2
💊

Get Verified Treatment

Receive ICAR/KVK-backed immediate actions, prevention steps, and warnings — in your language, via voice or text. We never invent advice.

💰
